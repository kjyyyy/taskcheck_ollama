# Construction Materials Guide - UK Standards

## Structural Materials

### Concrete
- **Types**: C20, C25, C30, C35, C40 (strength classes)
- **Applications**: Foundations, structural elements, slabs
- **UK Standards**: BS EN 206-1, BS 8500
- **Suppliers**: Hanson, CEMEX, Tarmac, Aggregate Industries
- **Cost Range**: £80-120 per cubic metre
- **Lead Time**: 1-3 days for ready-mix

### Steel
- **Types**: Mild steel, high-strength steel, stainless steel
- **Applications**: Structural frames, reinforcement, connections
- **UK Standards**: BS EN 10025, BS 4449
- **Suppliers**: British Steel, Tata Steel, ArcelorMittal
- **Cost Range**: £600-1200 per tonne
- **Lead Time**: 2-6 weeks for structural sections

### Timber
- **Types**: Softwood, hardwood, engineered timber (CLT, glulam)
- **Applications**: Framing, flooring, cladding, structural elements
- **UK Standards**: BS EN 14081, BS 5268
- **Suppliers**: James Latham, Arnold Laver, Travis Perkins
- **Cost Range**: £20-80 per square metre
- **Lead Time**: 1-2 weeks

## Insulation Materials

### Cavity Wall Insulation
- **Types**: Mineral wool, phenolic foam, PIR boards
- **Thermal Performance**: 0.18-0.22 W/m²K
- **UK Standards**: BS EN 13162, Building Regulations Part L
- **Suppliers**: Kingspan, Celotex, Rockwool
- **Cost Range**: £8-15 per square metre
- **Installation**: Professional installation required

### Roof Insulation
- **Types**: Mineral wool, PIR boards, spray foam
- **Thermal Performance**: 0.13-0.18 W/m²K
- **UK Standards**: BS EN 13162, Building Regulations Part L
- **Suppliers**: Kingspan, Celotex, Rockwool
- **Cost Range**: £12-25 per square metre
- **Installation**: During roof construction or retrofit

## Finishing Materials

### Plaster and Render
- **Types**: Gypsum plaster, cement render, lime render
- **Applications**: Internal walls, external facades
- **UK Standards**: BS EN 520, BS EN 998
- **Suppliers**: British Gypsum, Weber, Parex
- **Cost Range**: £15-30 per square metre
- **Application**: Professional application recommended

### Flooring
- **Types**: Hardwood, engineered wood, laminate, vinyl, carpet
- **Applications**: Residential and commercial flooring
- **UK Standards**: BS EN 13329, BS EN 14041
- **Suppliers**: Tarkett, Forbo, Interface
- **Cost Range**: £20-150 per square metre
- **Installation**: Varies by material type

## Roofing Materials

### Roof Tiles
- **Types**: Concrete tiles, clay tiles, slate
- **Applications**: Pitched roofs, heritage projects
- **UK Standards**: BS EN 490, BS EN 1304
- **Suppliers**: Marley, Redland, Sandtoft
- **Cost Range**: £25-80 per square metre
- **Installation**: Professional roofing contractor

### Flat Roofing
- **Types**: Built-up felt, single-ply membranes, liquid applied
- **Applications**: Flat roofs, balconies, terraces
- **UK Standards**: BS EN 13707, BS EN 13956
- **Suppliers**: Sika, Bauder, IKO
- **Cost Range**: £30-80 per square metre
- **Installation**: Specialist contractor required

## Windows and Doors

### Windows
- **Types**: uPVC, timber, aluminium, composite
- **Energy Performance**: A+ to C ratings
- **UK Standards**: BS EN 14351, Building Regulations Part L
- **Suppliers**: Anglian, Everest, Safestyle
- **Cost Range**: £300-1500 per window
- **Installation**: FENSA registered installer required

### Doors
- **Types**: Timber, uPVC, composite, steel
- **Security Standards**: PAS 24, Secured by Design
- **UK Standards**: BS EN 14351, Building Regulations Part M
- **Suppliers**: Jeld-Wen, Solidor, Rockdoor
- **Cost Range**: £200-2000 per door
- **Installation**: Professional fitting recommended

## Plumbing and Electrical

### Plumbing Materials
- **Pipes**: Copper, PEX, polybutylene
- **Fittings**: Compression, soldered, push-fit
- **UK Standards**: BS EN 1057, WRAS approval
- **Suppliers**: Wolseley, Plumb Center, Screwfix
- **Cost Range**: £5-50 per metre (pipes)

### Electrical Materials
- **Cables**: Twin and earth, SWA, fire-resistant
- **Consumer Units**: 17th/18th edition compliant
- **UK Standards**: BS 7671 (IET Wiring Regulations)
- **Suppliers**: Screwfix, Toolstation, CEF
- **Cost Range**: £1-10 per metre (cables)

## Sustainability and Green Materials

### Sustainable Options
- **Recycled Materials**: Recycled steel, reclaimed timber
- **Low Carbon**: Hempcrete, straw bale, rammed earth
- **Renewable**: Bamboo, cork, natural insulation
- **Certifications**: FSC, PEFC, BREEAM
- **Suppliers**: Various specialist suppliers
- **Cost Premium**: 10-30% above standard materials

## Material Selection Guidelines

### Project Type Considerations
- **Residential**: Focus on comfort, energy efficiency, aesthetics
- **Commercial**: Durability, fire resistance, maintenance
- **Industrial**: Strength, chemical resistance, safety
- **Heritage**: Traditional materials, conservation requirements

### Budget Planning
- **Low Budget**: Standard materials, bulk purchasing
- **Medium Budget**: Quality materials, some premium options
- **High Budget**: Premium materials, bespoke solutions

### Timeline Considerations
- **Quick Projects**: Readily available materials
- **Long-term Projects**: Materials with longer lead times
- **Seasonal Projects**: Weather-dependent materials

## Regulatory Compliance

### Building Regulations
- **Part A**: Structural safety
- **Part B**: Fire safety
- **Part C**: Site preparation and resistance to contaminants
- **Part L**: Conservation of fuel and power
- **Part M**: Access to and use of buildings

### Standards and Certifications
- **CE Marking**: European conformity
- **UKCA Marking**: UK conformity assessment
- **BBA Certificates**: British Board of Agrément
- **BRE Certification**: Building Research Establishment

## Supplier Selection Criteria

### Reliability Factors
- **Track Record**: Established suppliers with good reputation
- **Financial Stability**: Secure supply chain
- **Quality Assurance**: ISO 9001 certification
- **Technical Support**: Expert advice and after-sales service

### Cost Considerations
- **Bulk Discounts**: Volume purchasing benefits
- **Delivery Costs**: Transportation and handling
- **Storage Requirements**: On-site or off-site storage
- **Waste Management**: Disposal and recycling options
